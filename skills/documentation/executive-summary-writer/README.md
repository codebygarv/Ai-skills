# 👔 Executive Summary Writer

Translate dense technical documents into concise, high-impact executive briefs.

## What it does

Distills complex engineering postmortems, RFCs, and infrastructure investments into crisp 1-page summaries highlighting business impact, root causes, and ROI.

## When to use it

- Board reports and executive leadership updates.
- Executive postmortem briefings after major outages.
- Requesting budget for large technical migrations.

## Best for

- Executive communication & BLUF summaries
- Engineering leadership updates
- Incident business impact reports
