# 🗒️ Meeting Notes Distiller

Turn a wall of raw notes into what people actually need to know.

## What it does

Converts raw meeting notes/transcripts into decisions, action items (with owner and deadline where stated), and open questions — short enough to actually get reread.

## When to use it

- Right after a meeting, turning raw notes/transcript into a shareable summary.
- Someone missed a meeting and needs the outcome, not the full transcript.
- Standardizing summary format for a recurring meeting.

## Best for

- Post-meeting summaries
- Catching up absent teammates quickly
- Making sure action items don't get lost in a wall of discussion notes

## Example usage

> "Distill this meeting into a summary" (paste raw notes/transcript)

## Expected output

A Decisions list, an Action Items table (owner + deadline where known), and an Open Questions list — with optional brief context for non-obvious decisions.

## Limitations

- Won't invent an owner or deadline that wasn't stated or clearly implied — flags "unassigned" instead of guessing.
- Quality depends on the source notes actually capturing what was said — can't recover context that was never written down.
